We plan practice around school topics and your child’s current level.

Turkish

We work on reading comprehension, expression, writing and the topics covered at school, using short reviews, questions and extra practice.

Maths

We review topics and practise questions in line with their teacher’s lessons. We explore tricky points together and revisit areas that need more practice.

Their homework.
My support along the way.

First, we understand the task.

We look through the homework together and discuss where to start and what materials are needed.

I give them time to try.

I encourage them to think and solve it themselves, offering hints or another way to approach the question when needed.

Then we check together.

We review anything unfinished and plan short reviews or extra practice for difficult topics.

There is room
for play too.

We can agree with the family on school collection, walking home and companionship until you return.

Alongside schoolwork, we play and, when suitable, spend time in the park or outdoors. We consider tiredness and the need for breaks.
